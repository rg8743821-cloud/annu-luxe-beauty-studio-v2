Our Introduction

A Home Salon Built on Craft & Care

Annu's Luxe Beauty Studio started with a clear purpose: to bring the polish and precision of a premium salon straight into the homes of Indirapuram and Ghaziabad. What began as a small home-service practice has grown into a trusted name for bridal makeup, hair styling, skin treatments and nail artistry.

Every appointment is treated as an occasion — whether it's a wedding morning or a quiet Sunday facial. Our artists bring studio-grade tools, premium products and an eye for detail to every visit.

  • Personalised beauty consultations
  • Trained artists across makeup, hair, skin & nails
  • Strict hygiene & sanitisation standards
